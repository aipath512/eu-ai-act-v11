How It Works

Three paths into EU AI Act readiness, depending on what you need right now.

Path 1 — Recommended

🏢 Your Firm — Generate Your Pack

Build your organisation's own compliance pack, personalized with your firm's legal name and AI Act responsible person.

  1. Go to the Generator and enter your firm's legal name, responsible person, and email.
  2. Choose Markdown or Word format and your document language.
  3. Click Generate — all 70 documents (10 modules × 7 document types) are produced and delivered as a zip.
  4. Review the pack, or browse and download individual documents as needed.
Go to the Generator →
Path 2

👁 Demo — See a Complete Example First

Not ready to enter your own details yet? See the full document set with sample data before committing.

  1. Open the Demo — no signup required.
  2. Browse a complete example EU AI Act readiness pack: AI inventory, risk assessment, policies, registers, evidence records.
  3. When you're ready, move to the Generator to produce your own personalized version of the same documents.
View the Demo →
Path 3

💬 Ask — Understand Your Obligations First

Have a specific question about the EU AI Act before generating anything? Ask it directly.

  1. Type a question into the Ask box — in English or any of the supported EU languages.
  2. Get a grounded, cited answer pulled directly from Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.
  3. Use the quick-topic buttons (AI Literacy, High-Risk Classification, GPAI Obligations, Transparency, AI Inventory) to explore common areas.
