Frequently asked questions

How many free document packs can I generate?
Two free 70-document packs per email address. A third attempt from the same email is redirected to the partnership and distribution page for firms needing more.
What's included in the 70-document pack?
10 RRVI compliance modules × 7 document types each — Checklist, Declaration, Policy, Evidence, Procedure, Register, and Work Instruction — covering AI System Inventory, Risk Classification, AI Literacy, Prohibited Practices, High-Risk Assessment, Technical Documentation, Human Oversight, Data Governance, Logging & Monitoring, and Incident Reporting.
What information do I need to generate my pack?
Your firm's legal name, your AI Act responsible person's first and last name, and a work email — that's it.
What file formats are available?
Markdown (.md) or Word (.docx), selectable before you generate.
Can I browse individual documents before generating the full pack?
Yes — the document list lets you view or download any of the 70 documents individually once your firm name and responsible person are filled in.
Is my data stored?
Your email, firm name, and responsible person are kept so we can follow up on your compliance pack and enforce the free-tier limit.
What languages are the documents available in?
English is the default working language; French and Romanian are prioritized for further rollout.
What happens after I use my 2 free generations?
You're redirected to the Partnership & Distribution page, where single-firm and partner/reseller pricing is available.
Can I regenerate my pack later if my firm details change?
Paid packs (Single Firm tier) can be re-generated for 12 months; the free tier is limited to 2 total generations.
Who is the AI Act responsible person field for?
The individual your organization designates as accountable for EU AI Act compliance — this name is populated into all 70 generated documents as the named responsible party.
