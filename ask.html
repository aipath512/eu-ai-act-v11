Frequently asked questions

What is the Ask box for?
A conversational search tool that answers questions about EU AI Act obligations directly, with citations back to the regulation text.
What languages can I ask questions in?
English and the other EU languages the site supports — the system detects the question's language automatically.
Are the answers grounded in the actual regulation?
Yes — answers are generated from retrieved chunks of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 text and cite the specific articles they draw from.
What if the Ask box can't find a grounded answer?
It won't fabricate one — if no retrieved passage actually covers the question, it says so rather than generating an unsupported answer.
Can I ask about my own firm's specific situation?
The Ask box answers general regulatory questions; for firm-specific interpretation, the HELP service connects you with an expert.
Does Ask also cover the RRVI compliance documents, or just the law?
Both — it can answer practical 'how do I' questions using the RRVI document library alongside the regulation text itself.
Is there a cost to use the Ask box?
No — it's free to use, no signup required.
How current is the information Ask provides?
It's grounded in the official EUR-Lex text of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.
Can I ask about specific articles, like Article 50?
Yes — you can ask about any article number or topic, such as transparency obligations, AI literacy, or high-risk classification.
What's the difference between Ask and the Generator?
Ask answers questions about your obligations; the Generator produces your firm's actual 70-document compliance pack.
