Frequently asked questions

Why is the free generator limited to 2 packs per email?
The free tier is meant for a single firm to test its own compliance pack end to end — not for agencies generating packs for many different clients under one email. Two free generations are enough to evaluate the document quality and format before deciding whether to buy a single pack or apply as a partner.
What's included in the Single Firm pack (€149)?
All 70 RRVI compliance documents (10 modules × 7 document types — Checklist, Declaration, Policy, Evidence, Procedure, Register, Work Instruction), personalized with your firm's legal name and AI Act responsible person, delivered in Markdown or Word format, with the ability to re-generate the same pack anytime for 12 months.
Who is the Partner / Distribution tier for?
Consultancies, law firms, and platforms that need to generate RRVI packs for multiple client firms on an ongoing basis, rather than a single firm generating its own pack once.
Is white-labeling available?
Yes — partners can apply their own branding to the generated documents instead of the EU AI Act Ready™ branding, as part of the Partner / Distribution tier.
How is partner pricing structured?
Partner pricing is custom and volume-based, quoted after you apply through the partnership form with an estimate of how many client packs you expect to generate per month.
Is there a minimum client volume to qualify as a partner?
There's no fixed published minimum — pricing is quoted individually based on the volume and use case described in your application.
How fast is a partnership application reviewed?
Applications submitted through the form on this page are followed up by email directly from the EU AI Act Ready™ team.
Do partners get priority document generation?
Yes — the Partner / Distribution tier removes per-client generation caps and is prioritized over the free trial tier.
What format are the compliance documents delivered in?
Markdown (.md) or Word (.docx), selectable per generation, for both the Single Firm and Partner tiers.
Who do I contact about partnership or distribution?
Use the application form on this page, or email contact@5thelement.ai directly.
